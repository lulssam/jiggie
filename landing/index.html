Every walk, meal and dose. One record your vet can read.

Jiggie! turns the small daily things into a clinical history. Log in two taps, share the household, and hand your vet a proper record instead of a guess.

HOW IT WORKS

Set it up once. Then it's two taps a day.

  1. 1

    Add your dog

    Name, breed, age, colour. Twenty seconds, and the record starts.

  2. 2

    Invite the household

    Share one code. Everyone logs into the same history — no duplicate doses.

  3. 3

    Log as you go

    The + button covers walk, food, water, medicine and symptoms in two taps.
